          Kinetic Trust Protocol (KTP) - Repair Specification
                      draft-perkins-ktp-repair-00

Abstract

   This document specifies repair for the Kinetic Trust Protocol (KTP):
   what a breach is, what a reconciliation record is, and the rule that
   standing lost at a breach does not return without one.  Repair is
   required before re-entry whether or not any party has raised a
   dispute.  The same rule applies to the actor whose act breached, to
   the relationship the breach damaged, and to the rule under which
   breaches recur.

   The document also specifies the deployment's answer to breaches
   arriving faster than they can be repaired: the open breach stock,
   declared repair capacity, utilization against it, ambient breach as a
   source of environmental heat, and the tiers a zone may enter when
   repair falls behind, with what no tier may change.

1.  Introduction

   Every other specification in this set describes how authority is
   computed from current conditions and withdrawn when conditions no
   longer support it.  None describes how it comes back.  A system that
   can stop an actor and cannot return it has a door that only closes;
   the actor stops correctly, and then nothing happens.





Perkins                   Expires 16 April 2027                 [Page 2]

Internet-Draft                 KTP-REPAIR                   October 2026


   This document supplies the return arrow and the condition on it.
   After a breach, standing does not return because time passed, because
   the actor restarted, because a fresh proof was issued, or because no
   one complained.  It returns when a reconciliation record is anchored
   in the zone's audit chain, and it returns to a ceiling below where it
   stood.

   Repair is not conflict resolution.  Conflict resolution is invoked
   when a party carries a grievance to it.  Repair is required before
   re-entry whether or not anyone carried anything.  The two coexist: a
   party may dispute a breach under [KTP-GOVERNANCE] and [KTP-HUMAN],
   and the dispute's outcome is evidence for the record; the record is
   still required.

   Repair applies at three levels, and this document treats each: the
   actor whose act breached, whose standing is ceilinged until the
   record exists; the relationship the breach damaged, which the record
   names; and the rule under which breaches recur, which is itself in
   breach and is repaired by an amendment that cites the records that
   drove it.

3.  Terminology

   Standing: The accumulated measure of the agent-environment
   relationship that [KTP-CORE] carries as Base Trust (E_base) and
   evaluates, with the environment's present condition, as the Effective
   Trust Score.  Standing is the quantity a breach ceilings and
   reconciliation releases.

   Breach: The state an actor enters by its own act against the gate, as
   Section 4.2 (Entry) enumerates.  A Silent Veto is not a breach.
   Breach is a state of the actor's trajectory, recorded in the zone's
   audit chain, not a state of the environment.

   Reconciliation Record: A Flight Recorder record, anchored in the
   zone's audit chain, that names the breach it closes, each party by
   the terminator of its accountability chain, the residual harm, and an
   independent verifier.  Section 4.3 (The Record) specifies it.





Perkins                   Expires 16 April 2027                 [Page 3]

Internet-Draft                 KTP-REPAIR                   October 2026


   Re-entry Reduction (Δ): The declared fraction by which an actor's
   standing at the breach is reduced to set the ceiling at which the
   actor re-enters.  Section 4.4 (The Cost) specifies it.

   Breach Arrival (Z): The weighted count of breaches entering the
   zone's audit chain per aggregation window, whether or not any is
   reconciled.

   Repair Capacity (μ): The number of reconciliation records the zone's
   accountable roots can bring to anchor per aggregation window under
   sustained backlog, declared in the zone's published profile.  Roots
   produce and submit records; the Oracle anchors them in the chain.

   Open Breach Stock (U): The weighted count of breaches in the zone's
   audit chain for which no reconciliation record is anchored.

   Criticality (K): The zone's declared measure of how asymmetric the
   harm of carrying an unreconciled breach is in that zone.  A decision
   in the published profile, not a measurement.  It sets the reserve the
   zone must keep, 1/K.

   Ambient Breach (ρ): Utilization, the breach arrival over the repair
   capacity, Z/μ, reported with the zone's criticality as the breach
   index B = Kρ to the zone's environmental measurement as a source of
   adversarial pressure.

   Repair: Restoring standing, a relationship, or a rule after a breach.
   Reconciliation is one act of repair.  This is the sense in which the
   Open Trust Commons records a project's repair capability; this
   document is upstream of that use and the two do not diverge.

   Governing Authority: The party [KTP-GOVERNANCE] identifies as holding
   E-setting authority for the zone, which publishes the zone's profile
   and stands as the affected party where only the zone is affected.

4.  The Precondition

4.1.  Standing

   Standing lost at a breach does not return without reconciliation.  A
   breach, and the reconciliation that closes it, is an anchored record
   in the zone's audit chain; it persists by its anchor and not by its
   signatures, and it is a record only while its anchor verifies under
   the zone's current cryptographic profile.  An actor whose evaluation
   entered Breach *MUST NOT* have readiness re-established, and *MUST
   NOT* re-enter any tier at or above its tier at the breach, until the
   reconciliation record for that breach is anchored.  The breach
   attaches to the actor's trajectory: an agent spawned from it after



Perkins                   Expires 16 April 2027                 [Page 4]

Internet-Draft                 KTP-REPAIR                   October 2026


   the breach carries it, a Sponsorship Bond does not close it, and a
   zone that receives the actor measures the breach as its own exposure
   and never as reconciled.  Where the actor no longer exists, the
   obligation to reconcile rests with its lineage at the sponsor and
   with the chain's terminator.  Silence, the lapse of time, a restart,
   a fresh ordinary proof, a new sponsor, or the actor's ending is not
   reconciliation.

   Readiness is the readiness decision of [KTP-CORE] Section 7.4
   (Operational Readiness for the Requested Action); the tier is the
   tier of [KTP-ENFORCE]; the trajectory, the lineage, the sponsor and
   the terminator are those of [KTP-IDENTITY] and of [KTP-CORE]
   Section 5.1 (Base Trust (E_base)); anchoring is that of [KTP-AUDIT];
   the cryptographic profile is that of [KTP-CRYPTO].  Where the actor
   is a person, the trajectory is the person's signed acts and nothing
   else, and standing is the zone's measure of the person's acts under
   the protocol and bears on nothing outside it.  The party who owes
   reconciliation for an ended actor is the root that held authority
   over its lineage, as the sponsor and the chain's terminator are
   declared; a person who directed the actor under a grant without
   lineage-wide visibility, the authority to refuse, and the power to
   stop its next act is not by that direction the party.

4.2.  Entry

   An actor enters Breach by its own act, never by the gate's refusal.
   A consequential act executed, or presented for release, outside the
   signed decision it consumed or without one, is a breach.  An
   attestation withdrawn for cause against the actor, or a finding of
   misattestation, is a breach.  A deployment *MAY* declare further
   entry classes in its published profile; it *MUST* declare them for
   every actor alike and *MUST NOT* declare them for one.  A Silent Veto
   is not a breach, and an action the environment could not bear carries
   no obligation to reconcile.

   The first entry is an act whole: a release that matches its decision
   fragment by fragment and not as a whole is a breach of the actor.
   The second entry is already priced by [KTP-CORE] Section 5.1 (Base
   Trust (E_base)): a for-cause claim that does not survive scrutiny is
   misattestation and debits the claimant.  The first two entries are
   hard entries.  A profile-declared entry is a soft entry unless the
   profile declares it hard.  A breach record is contestable under the
   review route and the response deadlines the set already declares; a
   correction changes the authoritative evidence and requires a fresh
   evaluation, and never revives what was superseded, as [KTP-CORE]
   states for every correction.  A breach record that is wrong is fixed
   while it can still matter, not after.

4.3.  The Record

   A reconciliation record is a Flight Recorder record, anchored in the
   zone's chain, that names the breach it closes by its anchor, each
   party by the terminator of its chain, the residual harm, and the
   verifier.  The verifier *MUST* be independent of every party by the
   test this set states for an Independent Party, at the date of the
   record; the Oracle anchors the record and *MUST NOT* be its verifier.
   The record *MAY* declare the harm unrepairable; declaring it is
   compliant, omitting it is not.  The record carries no account of what
   was said; it states that the parties were present, who confirmed it,
   and what remains.

   The record also carries the breaching party's acknowledgment of the
   breach, by the terminator of its chain; a record that states presence
   and residual harm and no acknowledgment is incomplete and does not
   close the breach.  The Independent Party test is the one
   [KTP-CONFORMANCE] states for conformance claims, applied here to the
   parties of the record in place of the author and the implementer.
   The record type and its fields are specified in Section 6 (Records).
   A zone that cannot produce an independent verifier cannot produce a
   record, and standing does not return; this is the stated result, not
   a gap.

4.4.  The Cost

   A breach lowers a ceiling on standing.  At re-entry the actor's base
   standing *MUST NOT* exceed its standing at the breach reduced by a
   declared fraction Δ, and the ceiling lifts only by acts recorded
   after the reconciliation record, under the ordinary rail.  The
   deployment declares Δ in its published profile for each entry class.
   For a breach entered by the actor's act against a signed decision, or
   by withdrawal for cause or misattestation, Δ *MUST* be at least one
   tenth; a profile that declares less is non-conformant.  For a
   repeated breach on the same lineage, Δ *MUST* be no less than the Δ
   applied to the one before.  Re-entry restores the eligibility to earn
   standing and restores nothing else: an instrument withdrawn for cause
   stays withdrawn, the tier follows from the standing, and what was
   lost is earned again, not returned.

   The ceiling is a term of the same kind as the generation ceiling of
   [KTP-CORE] Section 5.1 (Base Trust (E_base)): a bound on base
   standing, not a contribution to it, and not a tier.  Because the tier
   is a readout of standing, no separate tier cap is required and none
   is specified; an implementation that caps the tier and leaves the
   standing untouched has applied no cost, because the ordinary rail
   restores the tier at the next evaluation.

4.5.  Required, Not Invoked

   Reconciliation is required before re-entry whether or not any party
   has raised a dispute; a deployment that re-enters an actor on the
   absence of complaint is non-conformant.  The obligation to seek
   reconciliation rests with the breaching party's lineage and
   terminator, never with the affected party, and an affected party's
   attendance is voluntary.  A zone *MUST* publish the route by which
   reconciliation is sought.  Where an affected party declines after
   that route has been followed, the verifier confirms the route was
   followed, the record carries the residual harm as the affected party
   declared it or, where they declared nothing, as undeclared and
   carried as unrepairable, and the record remains open to the affected
   party to attend and amend at any later time; declining is neither a
   veto on the record nor a closing of it.  Where no party other than
   the zone is affected, the zone's governing authority is the affected
   party, and the verifier *MUST* be independent of it.

   The route is published with the zone's profile.  Who attends depends
   on who was harmed.  Where the affected party is a person, attendance
   is voluntary; the review route of [KTP-HUMAN] is unchanged by this
   document and is not the reconciliation route, and a person is never
   required to attend.  Where the affected party is an agent, the
   terminator of its lineage attends in its place and is required to; a
   terminator who declines after the route has been followed has
   committed, against its own lineage, the breach that Section 5.1
   (Seeking Is an Act) names for a lineage that seeks and does not
   attend.

   The affected party's answer changes the course and not only the
   record.  The profile *MUST* declare how a residual harm the affected
   party declares unrepaired raises Δ above the floor for that breach; a
   declaration never lowers Δ, and a declaration is not a veto on the
   record or on re-entry.  A protection that held only when the affected
   party attended and persuaded would be a courtesy; the floor holds
   whether or not they attend, and what they declare raises it.

4.6.  The Rule

   Repair applies to the rule as it applies to the actor.  A rule,
   profile, or consequence class under which a breach pattern recurs, at
   the recurrence the deployment's published profile declares, is itself
   in Breach: the governing authority that set it is the breaching
   party, the lineages it bound are the affected parties, and its
   reconciliation record is the amendment, which *MUST* cite the
   reconciliation records that drove it.  An amendment that cites no
   reconciliation record is not repair, and a recurring pattern that
   amends nothing is not closed.  This specification's own repair runs

Internet-Draft                 KTP-REPAIR                   October 2026


   under its published decision process, which this document cites and
   does not restate.

   The mechanism by which an amendment is made and ratified inside a
   zone is not specified here; [KTP-CORE] names it among the limits of
   the set.

5.  Barrage and Ambient Breach

5.1.  Seeking Is an Act

   Seeking reconciliation is a signed act in the seeking party's
   trajectory.  A lineage that seeks reconciliation and does not attend
   has committed a further breach, of the hard class, against the record
   it sought.  The governing authority *MAY* queue sought
   reconciliations; a position in the queue changes no ceiling and re-
   establishes no readiness.

5.2.  Sponsorship Under Unreconciled Breach

   A root whose lineages carry unreconciled breaches above the count its
   published profile declares *MUST NOT* open a Sponsorship Bond until
   the count falls below it.  The declared count *MUST NOT* exceed the
   profile's declared repair capacity μ per window, and for hard entries
   the count *MUST NOT* be greater than one.  A bond opened in breach of
   this rule is void, and the agent it would have sponsored has no
   genesis.

   This is the only cost a lineage of disposable agents cannot avoid,
   and it is the lineage-summed bound that [KTP-CORE] names as owed
   under its limits.

5.3.  Declared Repair Capacity

   A zone *MUST* declare its repair capacity μ in its published profile:
   the number of reconciliation records its accountable roots can bring
   to anchor per aggregation window under sustained backlog, by entry
   class.  The declaration binds the declarer: the audit chain records
   every anchored record, and a declared capacity the chain does not
   bear out is a misdeclaration.  The effective repair capacity is the
   lesser of the declared capacity and the records actually anchored per
   window over the recovery window while the open breach stock was above
   zero; a zone that cannot anchor at its declared rate under backlog
   has its capacity revised to what its own chain demonstrates, and
   computes ambient breach against that.  A profile *MAY* declare a
   repairer interval, the time a repairer is given after bringing a
   hard-entry record to anchor before carrying the next; where it does,
   a record brought to anchor by a repairer within the interval still



Perkins                   Expires 16 April 2027                 [Page 8]

Internet-Draft                 KTP-REPAIR                   October 2026


   reduces the open breach stock and re-establishes what it re-
   establishes, and does not count toward demonstrated capacity.  A zone
   that spends its repairers cannot show the throughput as durable.  A
   zone that declares no repair capacity has unknown capacity, and
   unknown capacity is resolved restrictively under [KTP-CORE]
   Section 6.8 (Undefined Inputs): the zone is treated as saturated for
   the purposes of Section 5.4 (Ambient Breach) and consequential acts
   within it are subject to the supervision floor that applies when
   capacity is not known.  A declared capacity of zero is a declaration
   that the zone cannot host consequential acts while any breach is
   unreconciled.

   A zone *MUST* also declare its criticality K in the same profile.  K
   is a decision about the asymmetry of harm in that zone and is never
   derived from measurement.

5.4.  Ambient Breach

   Breach arrival Z is the weighted count of breaches entering the
   zone's audit chain per aggregation window: one for a hard entry, and
   the weight the profile declares for a soft entry.  No one lineage
   contributes to Z more than the share the profile declares for a
   single lineage; above that share its breaches still enter the open
   breach stock, still ceiling its standing and close its sponsorship,
   and raise the zone's arrival no further.  Arrival measures how many
   roots are breaching, not how loudly one is.  The open breach stock U
   is the weighted count of breaches with no anchored reconciliation
   record.  The stock changes only by arrival and by anchored records:
   nothing leaves it by the passage of time, a restart, the expiry of a
   window, a fresh proof, a new sponsor, or the actor's ending, and a
   breach carried into another zone under a federation agreement leaves
   it there as exposure and not here as repaired.

   Ambient breach ρ is the breach arrival over the declared repair
   capacity, Z/μ, taken over the aggregation window.  With the zone's
   declared criticality it is reported as the breach index B = Kρ. The
   zone's reserve is 1/K: while ρ is at or below it the zone holds the
   margin its criticality demands; above it the reserve is gone, whether
   or not the stock is growing; above one the stock necessarily grows;
   at two or above the stock grows faster than the whole of the zone's
   repair capacity.

   The breach index is reported to the zone's environmental measurement
   as a source feeding adversarial pressure, in the same way as the
   feeds [KTP-SENSORS] lists for that Risk Factor, windowed over the
   aggregation window and with the excess above one banked so that a
   lull does not clear it.  The weight the deployment gives it is the
   deployment's declared appetite under the prudence rail.  The signals



Perkins                   Expires 16 April 2027                 [Page 9]

Internet-Draft                 KTP-REPAIR                   October 2026


   from which arrival and stock are computed are those the catalogue
   already carries for obligation breaches and relational repair; this
   document introduces no signal.  The Effective Trust Score is
   downstream of the index and is never an input to it.

   The following hold for every conformant deployment, and the published
   vectors test them:

   1.  The open breach stock falls only when a reconciliation record is
       anchored.  Ambient breach falls when arrivals fall, and the
       banked excess does not clear and the stock does not fall for that
       reason.

   2.  Where zones nest under [KTP-ZONES], the breach index of a zone
       that encloses another is heard by the zone it encloses; calm in
       an enclosed zone is not heard by the zone that encloses it.

   3.  Above the reserve, the breach index raises the Risk Factor it
       feeds, to the ceiling the deployment declared for that feed.
       Above one, the stock is growing and the deployment *MUST* report
       it so.  The index does not by itself veto any action; the
       capacity check does.

   4.  A zone with no accountable root has a repair capacity of zero and
       is saturated whenever its open breach stock is above zero.

   5.  A person's acts contribute to arrival and stock as acts.  Ambient
       breach and the breach index are properties of the zone and *MUST
       NOT* be reported per person.

   6.  Breaches reproduce: a breach that draws a breach in answer, a
       rule that keeps producing them, and an adversary adapting to the
       backlog each raise the arrival that one breach ultimately causes.
       A deployment *MUST* measure the reproduction of breaches from its
       own chain, as the records amended to raise residual harm, the
       further breaches of seeking without attending, and the rule-
       repair records of Section 4.6 (The Rule), and *MUST* publish it
       with the profile.  A declared criticality is valid only while the
       reserve it sets keeps utilization below the point at which
       reproduction folds the repair queue; a profile whose criticality
       is not valid against its own measured reproduction is non-
       conformant until amended under Section 4.6 (The Rule).

   Informative reference forms, which a deployment is free to replace
   with its own, derived and checked in [ABT]: ρ = Z/μ over the window;
   B = Kρ; the stock as dU/dt = Z − R, where R is the records anchored
   in the window, R ≤ μ, and no term leaves the stock otherwise; the
   banked excess as C(t+Δt) = max(0, C(t) + (B − 1)Δt); with



Perkins                   Expires 16 April 2027                [Page 10]

Internet-Draft                 KTP-REPAIR                   October 2026


   reproduction g measured at idle, the queue folds at ρ = 1 − √g and
   the reserve is valid while K > 1/(1 − √g).  The derivation, the
   correction that produced the utilization form, the recovery
   predicate, and the falsification criteria are in [ABT]; conformance
   is to the behaviours above, not to the forms.

5.5.  Tiers and Crisis

   A zone's repair runs in one of three tiers, declared in advance with
   the profile, as follows.  Each tier is entered by the measure and
   left only by the recovery predicate below.

   Reserve gone: utilization above the zone's reserve and at or below
   one.  Nothing changes but the heat the index feeds.  The zone *MUST
   NOT* alter any rule of this document in this tier.

   Debt growth: utilization above one.  The zone *MAY* activate its
   declared contingency, and only what it declared: an order of
   reconciliation by residual harm and by criticality, declared in the
   profile and never chosen per case; verifiers obtained from a
   federation peer under a Federation Agreement, each independent of
   every party by the same test as any verifier; and a tightening of the
   admission of consequential acts, so that the zone generates fewer
   breaches while it repairs.  A contingency that is not declared in the
   profile is not available.

   Crisis: utilization at or above two, held over the recovery window.
   The zone *MAY* declare a repair emergency under [KTP-EMERGENCY], and
   only under it: its custodians, its activation threshold, its
   duration, and its stronger controls on amendment apply, and the
   zone's governing authority *MUST NOT* declare a repair emergency
   alone.

   No tier changes the following, and a declaration that touches any of
   them is non-conformant: the precondition of Section 4 (The
   Precondition); the floor on Δ; the independence of the verifier; that
   nothing leaves the open breach stock but an anchored record; that no
   actor re-enters on silence; that a record remains open to the
   affected party; and that nothing is reported per person.  A zone that
   cannot hold these in crisis *MUST* say so in its profile and *MUST
   NOT* host consequential acts.

   A zone leaves a tier only when the recovery predicate holds for the
   recovery window the profile declares: utilization below the tier's
   entry with a declared margin on the conservative estimate, the open
   breach stock at or below its declared target, the banked excess at
   zero, and records amended to raise residual harm and the further
   breaches of seeking without attending both below their declared



Perkins                   Expires 16 April 2027                [Page 11]

Internet-Draft                 KTP-REPAIR                   October 2026


   rates.  Crossing back below a threshold is not recovery.  A tier that
   was entered produces a rule-repair record under Section 4.6 (The
   Rule) naming what in the profile let it happen; the tier is itself a
   recurring pattern on the rule.

6.  Records

   A reconciliation record is a Flight Recorder record of type
   reconciliation, chained and anchored as [KTP-AUDIT] specifies for
   every record.  It carries:

   *  breach_anchor: the record hash of the breach record it closes.

   *  parties: the terminator of each party's accountability chain, as
      declared under [KTP-CORE] Section 5.1 (Base Trust (E_base)); for
      the breaching party, the lineage and the sponsor where the actor
      has ended.

   *  acknowledged_by: the terminator of the breaching party's chain,
      acknowledging the breach; an absent value leaves the breach open.

   *  residual_harm: the harm that remains, as the affected party
      declared it; the value unrepairable is permitted; the value
      undeclared is recorded where the affected party declared nothing
      and is carried as unrepairable; an absent value invalidates the
      record.

   *  verifier: the independent party and its declaration of
      independence at the date of the record.

   *  route_followed: the verifier's confirmation that the published
      route was followed, where an affected party declined.

   *  open_to: the affected parties who may attend and amend later.

   The record carries no transcript and no account of what was said.
   The breach record it closes is a Flight Recorder record of the type
   that recorded the breaching act or the withdrawal; this document adds
   no breach record type.

   Where a party to the record is a person, the record is personal data
   under [KTP-PRIVACY], is scoped to the zone, is never exported under a
   federation agreement, and is subject to the lawful disposition
   process [KTP-AUDIT] specifies.  Disposition does not reopen the
   ceiling: a disposed record remains a record for the purpose of the
   precondition by its anchor.

7.  Conformance

   The published vectors for this document are
   specifications/conformance/breach-reconciliation-v1.json.  They fix
   the decisions a conformant deployment produces at the boundaries this
   document names, including the tiers and what no tier may change, and
   define no formula for standing, Δ, the breach index, or reproduction.
   An implementation that claims conformance to this document carries
   the status label [KTP-CONFORMANCE] assigns to that claim.

   Three tests are published as cases that read the governed world
   rather than the decision record, in the public case repository with
   the status label proposed: the interval a human repairer is given
   before carrying the next case; the shape of the exchange a
   reconciliation record attests to; and a person who declines to
   attend.

   The requirements of [KTP-RELATIONAL] for repair ceremonies and
   witnesses are not replaced by this document.  Its witness requirement
   for a repair ceremony is satisfied by the verifier of Section 4.3
   (The Record); its repair process cites this document for the
   precondition.

8.  Security Considerations

   The threats this document is written against, and where each is
   answered:

   *  Identity shedding.  A breached actor spawns or splits and re-
      enters as a fresh agent.  The breach attaches to the trajectory,
      and descendants spawned after it carry it (Section 4.1).

   *  Sponsor laundering.  A new sponsor vouches the breached actor back
      in.  A bond does not close a breach, and a root above its declared
      unreconciled count cannot open one (Sections 4.1, 5.2).

   *  Zone hopping.  A breach in one zone, re-entry in another.  The
      breach is the measuring zone's anchored record; a receiving zone
      measures it as exposure and never as reconciled (Section 4.1).

   *  Fragment breach.  A release that matches its decision only
      fragment by fragment.  The act is judged whole (Section 4.2).

   *  Waiting it out.  Time, restart, fresh proof, new sponsor, the
      actor's ending.  None is reconciliation (Section 4.1).

Internet-Draft                 KTP-REPAIR                   October 2026


   *  Manufactured breach.  An adversary induces vetoes to flood the
      repair route.  A Silent Veto is not a breach; the first entry
      requires the actor's own signed act; the second is priced against
      the claimant (Section 4.2).

   *  Theatre.  A complete record with nothing paid. Δ has a floor on
      hard entries, the ceiling is on standing and not the tier, and it
      lifts only by later acts (Section 4.4).

   *  Holdout.  An affected party declines forever.  Attendance is
      voluntary and declining is not a veto; the record stays open to
      them (Section 4.5).

   *  Hollow return.  A decline is treated as closure.  The harm is
      carried as declared or as unrepairable, and the record is not
      closed (Section 4.5).

   *  Relentless barrage.  Breaches arrive faster than repair.  Seeking
      and not attending is a further breach; sponsorship closes above
      the declared count; the breach index raises the zone's heat so
      that fewer consequential acts are admitted; and a swarm whose
      breaches reproduce folds the repair queue below the declared
      capacity, which the measured reproduction and the validity of the
      declared criticality make visible (Section 5).

   *  Forged records after a signature break.  A record persists by its
      anchor and is a record only while the anchor verifies under the
      current profile (Section 4.1, [KTP-CRYPTO]).

   *  The orphan stop.  A breached actor with no way back.  The route is
      published, the obligation to seek it is on the breacher, and the
      tier below the ceiling keeps the heartbeat (Sections 4.5, 5.3).

   *  The repair loop as the weapon.  A root that breaches on purpose to
      drive the index, and so the heat, and so the vetoes, for everyone.
      One lineage's share of arrival is capped; the index measures
      breadth, not volume; the index never vetoes and feeds heat through
      a declared ceiling (Section 5.4).

   *  Collapse by exhaustion.  Declared capacity stays on paper while
      the roots who anchor wear out.  Effective capacity is the lesser
      of declared and demonstrated, so the zone's own chain revises the
      denominator before the index lies (Section 5.3).

   *  The crisis that drops the rules.  A zone in debt closes breaches
      by policy, lowers Δ, drains the stock.  No tier may change the
      precondition, the floor, the verifier's independence, the stock,
      re-entry on silence, or per-person reporting, and a repair
      emergency is declared only under the emergency specification's
      custodians (Section 5.5).

   The governing authority is itself constrained.  Where it stands as
   the affected party, the verifier is independent of it; where its rule
   recurs in breach, it is the breaching party (Sections 4.5, 4.6).

9.  Privacy Considerations

   The record names parties by terminator and carries no transcript.
   Attendance by an affected party is voluntary, and a person is never
   required to attend.  A person's acts contribute to breach arrival and
   stock as acts, and ambient breach is never reported per person.  A
   person's reconciliation record is zone-scoped personal data under
   [KTP-PRIVACY] and [KTP-AUDIT], and is subject to the Soul constraints
   of the zone, consent state and sovereignty included, which no measure
   in this document overrides.  Nothing in this document makes a
   person's standing a score outside the protocol.

10.  IANA Considerations

   This document has no IANA actions.

11.  Limits of This Specification

   This document binds re-entry and the rule, not the mechanism of
   amendment or the federation boundary.  [KTP-CORE] names both among
   the limits of the set: the mechanism by which an amendment is made
   and ratified inside a zone, and the reconciliation of a breach
   carried into another zone under a Federation Agreement.
